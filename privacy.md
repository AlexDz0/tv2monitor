# TV2Monitor Privacy Policy

TV2Monitor stores settings on your PC. It does not send your data to a server.

The app reads the list of connected devices and running programs only to run the rules you set. The names of those devices and programs are stored locally in the config file so the rules can run. They are not sent to a server.

Weather data is fetched from MET Norway only if you turn the feature on and accept the terms. The request includes your city name and a public support contact. No other personal data is sent.

The before-sign-in wake task stores the MAC address of your TV so it can send the wake packet. That address stays on your PC.

The seven-day trial timer is managed by the app. It is not managed by the Microsoft Store. Uninstalling the app removes the settings and the task.
